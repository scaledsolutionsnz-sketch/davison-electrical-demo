Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

This is placeholder wording for the demo website. Davison Electrical should replace it with its own privacy policy before going live.

1. Overview

Davison Electrical (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect

We only collect information you give us so we can quote and complete your work — typically your name, phone number, email address, and the address and details of the job. We don’t collect more than we need.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes.
  • To carry out and invoice electrical work.
  • To keep records required for compliance and warranty.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We only share it where needed to do the work (for example with a supplier or subcontractor on your job) or where required by law.

5. This website

This site does not include a contact form. When you tap the call or email buttons, your own phone or email app opens — nothing is submitted to or stored on this website. The embedded Google Map is served by Google under its own privacy terms.

6. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and to retain it only for as long as needed.

7. Your rights & contact

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you. Call us on 027 383 5008 or reach us via the contact section of our website.
