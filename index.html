What we do

Electrical work, done properly

Domestic, rural and commercial electrical across Oamaru and the wider Waitaki. If it’s wired, we’ll sort it: new work, repairs and upgrades.

Home wiring & rewires

New power points, extra circuits, dodgy old wiring made safe and full house rewires for villas and rentals across Oamaru.

Switchboard upgrades & RCDs

Old fuse boards swapped for a modern switchboard with RCD safety switches, so the power trips instead of the people.

LED lighting & downlights

Warmer, cheaper-to-run LED downlights, feature pendants, outdoor and security lighting, planned so the room actually feels right.

Fault-finding & repairs

Power that keeps tripping, a dead circuit or a burning smell, we track down the fault, explain it, and fix it right the first time.

New builds & renovations

Full electrical fit-out for new homes, extensions and reno projects, wired to plan, on programme, working alongside your builder.

EV chargers, solar & rural

Home EV charging points, solar tie-ins, sheds, pumps and farm power, sorted for lifestyle blocks and Waitaki farms.

About Davison Electrical

A local sparky you can actually get hold of

Davison Electrical is a locally owned electrical business based right here in Oamaru, run by Shaun Davison. We work across North Otago on everyday domestic jobs, rental compliance, farm and lifestyle-block power, and new builds, the kind of work that needs doing properly and left safe.

No call centre, no runaround. You deal with the person doing the work, get a straight answer on price and timing, and a job finished to standard.
